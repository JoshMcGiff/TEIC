- **Strong Turnout (30+ Participants)**: Over 30 researchers, students, software developers, and community contributors joined our inaugural session across the University of Limerick and online via Microsoft Teams.
- **Collaborating on Irish Tech Projects**: Several participants raised questions and shared ideas around actively collaborating across different Irish language and community technology projects.
- **Breaking Down Institutional Barriers**: Discussion emphasised the urgent need to break down institutional silos so that talent and efforts across universities and grassroots groups can connect and build together.
- **Keeping Taxpayer-Funded Research Open**: Strong consensus on uniting against publicly funded research being privatised by universities—preventing outcomes already paid for by the taxpayer from being locked behind paywalls or commercialised such that the public has to pay for them twice.
